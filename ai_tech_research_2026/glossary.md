# 中英术语表 · Bilingual Glossary

整理于 2026年10月 · 覆盖本文件夹里所有文档出现的核心概念

**怎么用**
* 每个词有四栏：中文、English、一句话解释、英文例句。例句是这些词在英文商业和科技写作里最常见的用法
* 建议先读一遍"创业与战略"和"商业与财务"两节，这是所有产业通用的语言；其他各节在读对应的产业文档时对照查
* 缩写第一次出现时，英文全称写在前面，缩写写在括号里，比如 annual recurring revenue (ARR)。在美国的商业对话里，很多缩写直接念字母

**关于连字符的说明**
按你的偏好，本文档和英文版文档的正文统一不用连字符。但在正式英文写作里，下面这些词作形容词时通常要加连字符，写邮件或论文时要注意：open source、real time、full duplex、long term、high risk、AI native、end to end、product market fit、usage based、outcome based、non dilutive、third party、first mover。

## 1. 创业与战略 · Startup and Strategy

| 中文 | English | 解释 | 例句 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 垄断 | monopoly | 在某个市场里没有直接竞争对手，能长期保持定价权 | Thiel argues that every great company is a monopoly in its own niche. |
| 完全竞争 | perfect competition | 很多公司卖几乎一样的东西，利润被竞争吃光 | Airlines operate close to perfect competition, so they earn very thin margins. |
| 从 0 到 1 | zero to one | Thiel 的说法：创造全新的东西，而不是复制已有的东西 | Copying a working model takes the world from 1 to n; inventing something new takes it from zero to one. |
| 秘密 | secret | Thiel 的概念：一个重要但很少有人相信的事实 | What important truth do very few people agree with you on? |
| 专有技术 | proprietary technology | 别人没有的技术，最好比第二名好 10 倍 | Proprietary technology should be at least ten times better than the closest substitute. |
| 网络效应 | network effects | 用的人越多，产品对每个人越有价值 | Payment networks benefit from strong network effects. |
| 双边网络效应 | two sided network effects | 平台连接两类用户，一边越多，另一边越愿意加入 | A marketplace with two sided network effects is hard to dislodge. |
| 规模经济 | economies of scale | 规模越大，单位成本越低 | Battery makers in China enjoy huge economies of scale. |
| 品牌 | brand | 用户心智里的位置，也是垄断的一种来源 | A trusted brand matters more in healthcare than in most industries. |
| 最后进入者优势 | last mover advantage | 价值来自长期现金流，成为最后一个、无法替代的玩家比第一个进场更重要 | Thiel cares more about last mover advantage than first mover advantage. |
| 先发优势 | first mover advantage | 第一个进入市场带来的好处 | First mover advantage means little if a better product arrives later. |
| 护城河 | moat | 保护公司不被竞争者攻破的长期优势 | Data that only you can collect is a strong moat. |
| 滩头市场 | beachhead market | 先拿下的那个小而集中的市场，之后再向外扩张 | Facebook used Harvard as its beachhead market. |
| 不公平优势 | unfair advantage | 别人难以复制的个人或团队优势 | Being a heavy user of AI agents could be your unfair advantage. |
| 风口 | hot trend; fad | 短期内很热、但可能很快退潮的机会 | Robot rentals for events were a fad that cooled within a year. |
| 窗口期 | window of opportunity | 一个机会能做成的有限时间段 | The window of opportunity closes once platforms build the feature themselves. |
| 颠覆 | disruption | 新进入者用更便宜或更简单的方案取代老玩家 | Thiel warns founders not to obsess over disruption. |
| 交集市场陷阱 | defining the market as an intersection | 把自己的市场描述成几个小市场的交集，假装没有竞争 | "The only British restaurant in Palo Alto" defines a market as an intersection. |
| 最小可行产品 | minimum viable product (MVP) | 能验证核心假设的最简版本产品 | We can ship a minimum viable product in six weeks. |
| 产品市场契合 | product market fit (PMF) | 产品真正满足了一个市场的强需求 | Retention is the clearest signal of product market fit. |
| 客户访谈 | customer interviews; customer discovery | 通过和潜在客户聊天来验证需求 | Do twenty customer interviews before writing any code. |
| 单位经济模型 | unit economics | 每个客户或每笔交易能赚多少钱 | The unit economics work if a clinic pays 299 dollars a month. |
| 获客成本 | customer acquisition cost (CAC) | 获得一个新客户平均花多少钱 | Our customer acquisition cost is too high for a cheap subscription. |
| 客户终身价值 | lifetime value (LTV) | 一个客户在整个合作期间带来的总收入或利润 | A healthy business keeps lifetime value well above acquisition cost. |
| 转化率 | conversion rate | 访问者中最终付费或下单的比例 | Referrals from ChatGPT convert at a higher rate than search traffic. |
| 留存 | retention | 用户在一段时间后还在使用的比例 | Strong retention matters more than fast signups. |
| 切换成本 | switching costs | 客户换掉你要付出的代价 | Hospitals face high switching costs once they adopt an EHR. |
| 纵向整合 | vertical integration | 一家公司同时控制产业链的多个环节 | UnitedHealth is highly vertically integrated through Optum. |
| 平台风险 | platform risk | 依赖的平台改规则或自己做同类产品的风险 | Building only on one model provider creates platform risk. |
| 套壳产品 | wrapper | 只在大模型外面加一层界面的产品 | Investors worry that a thin wrapper has no moat. |
| 创始人 | founder | 创办公司的人 | The founder spent two years in the industry before starting. |
| 联合创始人 | cofounder | 一起创办公司的人 | Look for a cofounder with deep industry knowledge. |
| 融资轮次 | funding round | 公司一次融资的阶段 | The company closed its funding round in March. |
| 种子轮 | seed round | 最早期的正式融资 | They raised a 27 million dollar seed round. |
| A 轮 | Series A | 种子轮之后的第一轮机构融资 | DG Matrix raised a 60 million dollar Series A. |
| 估值 | valuation | 投资人认为公司值多少钱 | The round valued the startup at 12 billion dollars. |
| 投后估值 | post money valuation | 本轮融资完成后的公司估值 | The post money valuation was 965 billion dollars. |
| 非稀释性资助 | non dilutive funding | 不需要出让股份的资金，比如政府资助 | The accelerator offers 100,000 dollars of non dilutive funding. |
| 加速器 | accelerator | 帮助早期创业公司的短期项目 | MassRobotics runs a 13 week accelerator for hardware startups. |
| 首次公开募股 | initial public offering (IPO) | 公司第一次在证券交易所上市 | Fervo went public through an IPO in May. |
| 退出信号 | exit signal; warning sign | 提醒你应该撤出一个风口的迹象 | Falling prices are the clearest warning sign that a fad is ending. |

## 2. 商业与财务 · Business and Finance Basics

| 中文 | English | 解释 | 例句 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 市场规模 | market size | 一个市场每年总共有多少钱 | Market size alone does not tell you where the profits are. |
| 潜在市场总量 | total addressable market (TAM) | 理论上能拿到的最大市场 | Founders often overstate their total addressable market. |
| 利润池 | profit pool | 一个产业的利润集中在哪里 | Banking is the largest profit pool in the world. |
| 价值链 | value chain | 从原材料到最终用户的每一个环节 | Profits in the AI value chain are concentrated in chips. |
| 产业链 | supply chain; industry chain | 和价值链类似，中文更常用 | China dominates the solar supply chain. |
| 上游 | upstream | 产业链靠近原材料的一端 | Oil exploration is an upstream business. |
| 下游 | downstream | 产业链靠近最终用户的一端 | Gas stations are downstream. |
| 收入 | revenue | 卖东西收到的钱 | NVIDIA's revenue doubled year over year. |

| 净利润率 | net margin | 净利润占收入的比例 | Grocery retailers run net margins of about 1 percent. |
| 年化收入 | annualized revenue; run rate | 用最近一段时间的收入推算一年的收入 | The company reached a 65 billion dollar run rate. |

| 运营开支 | operating expenses (opex) | 日常运营的花费 | Salaries are the largest part of operating expenses. |
| 市值 | market capitalization (market cap) | 上市公司股票的总价值 | NVIDIA has the largest market cap in the world. |
| 订阅 | subscription | 按月或按年付费使用 | Most software is sold as a subscription. |
| 按人头收费 | per seat pricing | 按用户账号数量收费 | Agents make per seat pricing less meaningful. |
| 按用量收费 | usage based pricing | 按使用量收费 | Model APIs use usage based pricing per million tokens. |

| 积压订单 | backlog | 已经签约但还没交付的订单 | GE Vernova's gas turbine backlog reached 116 gigawatts. |
| 交期 | lead time | 从下单到交货要等多久 | Large transformers have lead times of up to four years. |
| 产能过剩 | overcapacity | 生产能力远超需求 | Overcapacity has pushed solar prices below cost. |
| 价格战 | price war | 竞争者不断降价抢市场 | Chinese solar makers are stuck in a price war. |
| 内卷 / 反内卷 | involution / anti involution | 中国语境下的恶性竞争 / 政府推动减少恶性竞争 | Beijing launched an anti involution campaign in the solar industry. |
| 集中的市场 | concentrated market | 少数几家公司占大部分份额 | Chip manufacturing is a highly concentrated market. |
| 分散的市场 | fragmented market | 很多小公司，没有谁占大头 | Construction is one of the most fragmented markets. |
| 付款方与使用者 | payer and user | 付钱的人和使用产品的人 | In healthcare, the payer and the user are usually different people. |
| 商业模式 | business model | 公司怎么赚钱 | The business model depends on recurring revenue. |
| 中间商 | middleman; intermediary | 在买卖双方之间赚差价或手续费的角色 | PBMs act as middlemen between drug makers and insurers. |
| 循环交易 | circular deals; circular financing | 钱在少数几家公司之间来回流动 | Critics point to circular deals between chipmakers and AI labs. |

| 卖铲子 | selling picks and shovels | 在热潮里卖工具给参与者，而不是自己去淘金 | NVIDIA is the classic picks and shovels play of the AI boom. |

| 购买力平价 | purchasing power parity (PPP) | 按各国实际购买力调整后的比较方式 | China is the largest economy by purchasing power parity. |
| 同比 | year over year (YoY) | 和去年同一时期相比 | Revenue grew 106 percent year over year. |
| 环比 | quarter over quarter (QoQ) | 和上一季度相比 | Margins improved quarter over quarter. |
| 年复合增长率 | compound annual growth rate (CAGR) | 多年平均每年的增长率 | The market is growing at a 15 percent CAGR. |
| 财报 | earnings report | 上市公司定期公布的财务结果 | Investors are waiting for the third quarter earnings report. |
| 指引 | guidance | 公司对未来业绩的预测 | NVIDIA's guidance for next quarter is 108 billion dollars. |
| 年报 | annual report; Form 10 K | 上市公司的年度报告（美国叫 10 K） | Read the risk factors section of the annual report. |
